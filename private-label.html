Our mill is located at 119/6, Highway, Village Daha, Madanpur, Karnal 132001, Haryana, India. We began in 1978 and have a daily production capacity of 230 metric tons across three production units.

Rice products we manufacture

We manufacture 1121, 1509, Traditional, 1401, Pusa and 1718 Basmati, along with Sugandha, Sharbati, PR 11, Parmal and Sona Masoori Raw Rice. Residue-controlled Raw, Steam, Sella and Golden Sella options are also available.
